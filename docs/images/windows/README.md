# Glider for Windows

[All galleries](../)

Screenshots of Glider for Windows ("Glider/WIN 4.01", 1994), the release the port's "Windows look" will follow: its palette now, its art later.
They were captured from the archive.org in-browser emulator (DOSBox running Windows 3.1), which runs Windows in 256 colors; [`docs/versions.md`](../../versions.md) describes the release and what these show about its palette.

Click a picture to see it at full size.

**The House, room 3**, with the air over the vents visible.

![The House, room 3, in Glider for Windows](room-03-under-me.png)

**Room 4:** a mirror, a shelf, a cabinet and a battery.

![Room 4 in Glider for Windows](room-04-looking-at-you.png)

**Room 5:** a window with lightning, a balloon, and a floating score.

![Room 5 in Glider for Windows](room-05-youre-invited.png)

**The high scores**, in the Mac's layout with another font.

![The high-score table in Glider for Windows](high-scores.png)

**Name entry for a new high score**, a dialog of the Windows version's own.

![The high-score name entry in Glider for Windows](high-score-name-entry.png)

**Sound Settings.** Windows splits the Mac's Music setting in two: "Music" for the jingle on entering a room and "Bass Beat" for the bass during play. Bass Beat is off unless the player turns it on; the port will add the checkbox on by default, as the Mac plays the bass.

![The Sound Settings dialog in Glider for Windows](sound-settings.png)

## The object sheet

Each picture below is three parts of the object sheet side by side, enlarged three times: the Mac's color art of 4.04 (the same as 4.02's) in Windows' colors, Glider/WIN's, and 4.05's in Windows' colors. Glider/WIN took its sprites from the sheet of 4.04, before 4.05 moved its graphics to word boundaries; see [`docs/versions.md`](../../versions.md) for the whole comparison.

**The banners.** The top right of the sheet: on the Mac the crumpled glider, in Glider/WIN the banners the paper planes tow, new.

![Banners](sheet-banners-4.04-win-4.05.png)

**The flight recorder.** Glider/WIN's buttons for recording and playing back a flight, and the "G" of "Game Over", moved here from its column.

![Flight recorder buttons](sheet-flight-recorder-4.04-win-4.05.png)

**The wastebasket.** The same in all three, with the crumpled glider beside it in Glider/WIN.

![Wastebasket](sheet-wastebasket-4.04-win-4.05.png)
